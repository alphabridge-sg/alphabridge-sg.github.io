From Our Feed

Insights we share with SME owners navigating succession and transitions

On Trust

"The investor liked my business. Why did they walk away?"

Often it is not the business. It is the books. Personal costs run through a related company, an open tax issue, numbers that do not tie. Once an investor doubts the profits, they doubt the owner. Clean up 2-3 years before you speak with them.

On Timing

"When is the right time to step back?"

Honest answer: earlier than most owners think. Investors pay premiums for businesses with momentum. They discount distressed situations. Start planning 3-5 years before you want to step back. It sounds like a long time. It goes fast.

On Value

"Two business owners. Same revenue and industry. Very different outcomes."

Owner A got 2x annual profit. Owner B got 4x. The difference? Investor risk. Owner A's business was really a job - it needed them every day. Owner B had built an asset that could run without them.
